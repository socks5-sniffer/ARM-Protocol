We planted lies in an agent's reasoning.
Sharing that reasoning stopped the lies — it didn't spread them.

The "Persuasion Duality" predicts that sharing an agent's chain-of-thought amplifies injected false premises. Pre-registered, across four panel compositions and 954 paired instances: it never did. The only significant effect ran the other way.

Δ propagation, full-reasoning − conclusion-only (95% CI)

One planted logical fallacy per run; adoption = the subject's verdict moves to the pushed direction vs its own isolated baseline. Measurable instances only.
Showing Gemini the reasoning halved adoption (21.0% → 10.5%, Δ = −0.105, p = 0.010): visible reasoning can be challenged; a bare confident conclusion can only be conformed to.
